LocoMart Brunei Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

LocoMart Brunei ("LocoMart", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how LocoMart collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the LocoMart mobile application and related marketplace services.

By using LocoMart, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use LocoMart, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account credentials and authentication information
  • User account identifiers

Buyer Information

  • Delivery or contact information provided when placing an order
  • Transaction and order history
  • Wishlist and cart-related information

Seller Information

  • Business or store name
  • Store information
  • Business contact information
  • Product listings and product information
  • Store logos and product images
  • Other information provided when registering or managing a seller account

User-Uploaded Content

Depending on the features you use, you may upload:

  • Product images
  • Store logos
  • Payment proof
  • Feedback screenshots
  • Other files or information voluntarily submitted through the application

Device and Technical Information

We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, and improve the application, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system and platform information
  • Application version
  • Technical and diagnostic information

Location Information

LocoMart may access location information only where the application requests permission and you grant that permission. Location information will be used only for the purposes described to you when the permission is requested.

If a feature does not require location information, LocoMart will not request location access for that feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security
  • Provide marketplace services
  • Allow buyers and sellers to interact through the platform
  • Display product and store information
  • Process and manage orders and transactions
  • Facilitate payment and payment-proof processing
  • Facilitate delivery and communication between buyers and sellers
  • Allow sellers to manage their stores and products
  • Provide customer support and respond to feedback
  • Maintain, operate, and improve the application
  • Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Protect the security and integrity of the platform
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our Terms & Conditions

We will use personal information only for legitimate purposes related to operating, securing, and improving LocoMart and providing its services.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared when necessary to provide LocoMart's services, including:

With Other LocoMart Users

Certain information may be visible to other users when necessary for marketplace functionality.

For example, when an order is placed, relevant buyer and seller information may be shared to facilitate the transaction, communication, and delivery.

With Sellers

Information necessary to fulfill an order may be provided to the relevant seller.

With Buyers

Information associated with a seller, store, or product listing may be displayed to buyers as part of normal marketplace functionality.

With Service Providers

LocoMart uses third-party services to operate and maintain the application. These services may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide functionality, security, storage, authentication, and other platform services.

LocoMart uses services provided by Google Firebase, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage

These services may process and store information necessary for LocoMart's operation.

Legal Requirements

Information may be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, court order, government request, or to protect the rights, safety, security, and integrity of LocoMart, its users, or others.

4. User-Uploaded Content

Users may voluntarily upload content such as product images, store logos, payment proof, feedback screenshots, and other information necessary to use particular LocoMart features.

Uploaded content may be stored using third-party cloud services used by LocoMart, including Firebase services.

Users are responsible for ensuring that content they upload does not violate applicable laws, the rights of others, or LocoMart's Terms & Conditions.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, misuse, or destruction.

LocoMart uses security measures provided by its technology and cloud service providers, including authentication and access controls.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to:

  • Provide and maintain LocoMart services
  • Complete transactions and orders
  • Maintain account and transaction records
  • Resolve disputes
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain platform security
  • Comply with legal or regulatory obligations
  • Enforce agreements and protect our legal rights

When information is no longer reasonably required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or anonymize it where appropriate.

7. Your Rights

Subject to applicable laws and legitimate operational requirements, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information
  • Request information about how your data is handled
  • Withdraw permissions previously granted to the application where applicable
  • Contact us with privacy-related questions or concerns

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

8. Account Deletion

LocoMart provides users with a way to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

Users may request account deletion through the account-deletion functionality provided by LocoMart or by contacting us using the privacy contact information provided below.

When an account deletion request is received, LocoMart will delete or anonymize associated personal information where reasonably possible and where retention is not required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate purposes.

Certain records may therefore remain for a limited period where retention is legally required or reasonably necessary for the purposes described above.

9. Children's Privacy

LocoMart is not intended for individuals who are below the minimum age required to use the service under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to LocoMart in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

10. Third-Party Services

LocoMart may use third-party services necessary to operate the application, including cloud storage, authentication, database, analytics, security, and other technical services.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

LocoMart does not control the privacy practices of third-party service providers. Users should review the relevant third-party privacy policies where appropriate.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to LocoMart's services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

Where appropriate, significant changes may also be communicated through the application or other reasonable means.
