BRUNEI SHOPPER PATH
Simple, Transparent & Local
Shop verified vendors across all four Brunei districts with seamless manual bank transfer validation.
🛍️
👤
1. Account & Profile Setup
▼
1
Brunei Mobile Verification
Enter your active +673 mobile (+6/3/8) or email.
LocoMart delivers a swift SMS verification PIN to ensure secure buyer identity.
2
Set Default Kampung & District
Head to Settings → Delivery Addresses.
Pre-select your primary district (Brunei-Muara, Tutong, Belait, or Temburong) plus house number and Simpang for accurate runner rates.
🔍
2. Browsing & Smart Search
▼
1
District-Specific Browsing
Filter sellers by nearby proximity to minimize dispatch runner fees and pickup wait times.
2
MSME & Halal Badges
Look for the certified Brunei local producer icon to support homegrown enterprises and verified quality standards.
3
Wishlist & Favorites
Tap the ♡ icon on any item card to bookmark it for later purchase or flash discount notifications.
🛒
3. Cart & Multi-Seller Orders
▼
1
Grouped by Seller
Items from different sellers appear in separate groups in your cart.
Each group is checked out independently.
2
Stock Validation
Stock limits are automatically enforced. You can't add more than what's available.
3
Self-Purchase Prevention
You cannot purchase your own products as a seller.
💳
4. Checkout & Payment
▼
1
Cash on Delivery (COD)
Pay when the product is delivered.
No upfront payment needed.
Wait for seller to accept your order.
2
Bank Transfer
Transfer the amount to the seller's bank account.
Use your Order ID as the reference.
Upload proof of payment.
Wait for seller to verify.
📦
5. Order Tracking
▼
1
Viewing Orders
Go to Profile → My Orders.
Or tap the receipt icon on the homepage.
2
Order Statuses
Pending Payment — waiting for payment
Pending Verification — proof uploaded
Proof Rejected — re-upload needed
Waiting for Seller — waiting for seller (COD)
Pending Delivery — accepted, waiting for delivery
Completed — order delivered
3
Re-uploading Payment Proof
If rejected, you'll see the reason.
Tap "Re-upload Proof".
Select a new image and upload.
❤️
6. Wishlist
▼
1
Adding to Wishlist
Tap the heart icon on any product. You must be signed in.
2
Managing Wishlist
View: Profile → Wishlist
Add to cart: tap "Add"
Remove: tap the X
Clear all: tap "Clear All"
🆘
7. Troubleshooting
▼
1
Common Issues
Can't sign in → check email/password, reset if needed
Email not verified → check spam, resend verification
Cart empty after login → pull down to refresh
Product out of stock → seller will restock or cancel
Payment proof rejected → check reason, re-upload
Order not received → contact seller via order details
BRUNEI SELLER PATH
Grow Your Business Locally
List products, manage orders, and reach customers across all four Brunei districts.
🏪
📝
1. Becoming a Seller
▼
1
Requirements
Verified LocoMart account
Accept Terms & Conditions
Provide business information
2
Registering Your Business
Go to Profile → My Business.
Tap "Register Your Business".
Fill in: Store Name, Category, Contact Number, Description.
Select Payment Methods (COD and/or Bank Transfer).
If Bank Transfer: enter bank details.
Accept Terms & Conditions.
Tap "Register My Business".
📦
2. Managing Products
▼
1
Adding a Product
In Seller Hub, tap "Add Product".
Upload up to 5 images (required).
Fill in: Name, Description, Category, Price, Stock.
Optionally add Brand and Sale Price.
Tap "Publish Product".
2
Product Statuses
Active — visible to buyers
Inactive — hidden from buyers
Sold Out — stock is 0
3
Editing & Activating
Edit: tap "Edit" to update details
Deactivate: tap "Deactivate" to hide
Activate: tap "Activate" to show
View in Store: see how buyers see it
🧾
3. Managing Orders
▼
1
Order Statuses & Actions
Waiting for Seller → Accept or Decline
Pending Payment → wait for buyer proof
Pending Verification → Confirm or Reject proof
Proof Rejected → wait for re-upload
Pending Delivery → deliver the order
Completed → order finished
2
Accepting an Order (COD)
Open the order. Tap "Accept Order".
Status becomes "Pending Delivery".
Deliver the product.
3
Confirming Payment (Bank Transfer)
Buyer uploads proof of payment.
Review the proof.
Tap "Confirm Payment" if valid.
Or tap "Reject Proof" with a reason.
⚙️
4. Store Settings
▼
1
Editing Store Information
Seller Hub → Store Settings
Edit: Store Name, Category, Description
Update: Contact Number and Email
2
Store Logo
Tap "Store Logo".
Choose Camera or Gallery.
Upload your logo.
Logo appears on your store and product pages.
💰
5. Payments & Payouts
▼
1
How Payments Work
COD: you receive cash directly from buyer
Bank Transfer: buyer transfers to your bank account
2
Verifying Bank Transfers
Buyer transfers money.
Buyer uploads proof.
You verify the proof.
Confirm payment if valid.
⚖️
6. Seller Rules & Policies
▼
1
Product Rules
Products must be legal in Brunei
No counterfeit products
No prohibited items
Accurate descriptions and images required
2
Consequences
Listing removal
Account suspension
Permanent ban
🆘
7. Troubleshooting
▼
1
Common Issues
Can't upload images → check internet
Product not showing → check if Active
Order stuck → check status and take action
Payment not received → reject proof with reason
Store not visible → verify email and complete registration
