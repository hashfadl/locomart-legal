BUYER GUIDE
Shop Local, Shop Easy
Browse local sellers, add to cart, and pay with Cash on Delivery or Bank Transfer.
🛍️
👤
1. Account Setup
▼
1
Creating an Account
Open the LocoMart app.
Tap "Sign In" or "Create Account".
Enter your full name, email, password, and phone number (optional).
Accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
Tap "Create Account".
2
Verifying Your Email
After signing up, check your email inbox.
Click the verification link in the email.
Return to the app and tap "I've Verified / Refresh".
Check your spam folder if you don't see the email.
3
Signing In
Open the app and tap "Sign In".
Enter your email and password.
Tap "Sign In".
4
Forgot Password
On the Sign In screen, tap "Forgot Password?".
Enter your registered email.
Check your inbox for the reset link.
Follow the link to create a new password.
🔍
2. Browsing Products
▼
1
Homepage
Promo carousel at the top with announcements
Main categories below the carousel
Product grid under "For You"
Tap "Load More" at the bottom for more products
Pull down to refresh the product list
2
Browsing Categories
Tap a category card on the homepage
Or tap "Categories → See All" for the full list
Categories include: F&B, Clothing, Accessories, Beauty, Electronics, Shoes, Furniture, Sports, Books, Toys, Services, and Other
3
Searching for Products
Tap the search bar at the top
Type keywords like "shoes" or "Nike"
Filter by category
Sort by Newest, Price Low to High, or Price High to Low
Toggle "In Stock Only" to hide sold-out items
4
Product Details
Tap any product to view full details
Swipe images to see all product photos
View seller store information
Check stock availability
Tap the store name to visit their store page
🛒
3. Shopping Cart
▼
1
Adding to Cart
Tap "Add to Cart" on the product page
Or tap the cart icon on a product card
A confirmation message appears
2
Managing Your Cart
Increase quantity: tap +
Decrease quantity: tap –
Remove item: swipe left or tap X
Select items: tap the checkbox
Select all: tap "Select All" at the top
3
Important Notes
Items from different sellers are grouped separately
You cannot purchase your own products
Stock limits are enforced automatically
💳
4. Checkout & Payment
▼
1
Starting Checkout
In your cart, select the items you want.
Tap "Checkout".
Review the summary grouped by seller.
Tap "Start Checkout".
2
Cash on Delivery (COD)
Pay when the product is delivered
No upfront payment needed
Wait for seller to accept your order
3
Bank Transfer
Transfer the amount to the seller's bank account.
Use your Order ID as the reference.
Upload proof of payment (screenshot or photo).
Wait for seller to verify.
4
Multi-Seller Orders
If your cart has items from multiple sellers
You'll complete checkout once per seller
Each order is processed independently
📦
5. Order Tracking
▼
1
Viewing Orders
Go to Profile → My Orders
Or tap the receipt icon on the homepage
2
Order Statuses
Pending Payment — waiting for your payment
Pending Verification — proof uploaded, awaiting seller
Proof Rejected — re-upload needed
Waiting for Seller — waiting for seller (COD)
Pending Delivery — accepted, waiting for delivery
Completed — order delivered
Cancelled / Declined — order ended
3
Cancelling an Order
COD: can cancel if not yet accepted by seller
Bank Transfer: can cancel in "Pending Payment" or "Proof Rejected"
Tap "Cancel Order" in order details
4
Re-uploading Payment Proof
If rejected, you'll see the reason
Tap "Re-upload Proof"
Select a new image and upload
❤️
6. Wishlist
▼
1
Adding to Wishlist
Tap the heart icon on any product
You must be signed in
2
Managing Wishlist
View: Profile → Wishlist
Add to cart: tap "Add"
Remove: tap the X
Clear all: tap "Clear All"

7. Profile & Settings
▼
1
Editing Profile
Settings → Edit Profile to change your name
Name change: once every 24 hours
2
Contact Details
Settings → Contact Details
Update your phone number for deliveries
3
Feedback & Support
Settings → Feedback & Support
Send feedback, report bugs, attach screenshots

8. Account & Security
▼
1
Email Verification
Required for: placing orders, wishlist, seller registration
Check spam folder if not received
2
Password Security
Use a strong password (8+ characters)
Never share your password
Sign out on shared devices
3
Guest Mode
Browse products without signing in
Guest cart merges with your account on sign in
🆘
9. Troubleshooting
▼
1
Common Issues
Can't sign in → check email/password, reset if needed
Email not verified → check spam, resend verification
Cart empty after login → pull down to refresh
Product out of stock → seller will restock or cancel
Payment proof rejected → check reason, re-upload
Order not received → contact seller via order details
SELLER GUIDE
Grow Your Business
List products, manage orders, and reach customers across Brunei.
🏪
📝
1. Becoming a Seller
▼
1
Requirements
Verified LocoMart account
Accept Terms & Conditions
Provide business information
2
Registering Your Business
Go to Profile → My Business.
Tap "Register Your Business".
Fill in: Store Name, Category, Contact Number, Description.
Select Payment Methods (COD and/or Bank Transfer).
If Bank Transfer: enter bank name, account holder name, and account number.
Accept the Terms & Conditions.
Tap "Register My Business".
3
Accessing Seller Dashboard
Profile → My Business → Go to Seller Center
Or tap "Seller Hub" from your profile
📦
2. Managing Products
▼
1
Adding a Product
In Seller Hub, tap "Add Product".
Upload up to 5 images (required).
Fill in: Name, Description, Category, Price, Stock.
Optionally add Brand and Sale Price.
Tap "Publish Product".
2
Viewing Products
Seller Hub → My Products
Shows all products (active and inactive)
3
Product Statuses
Active — visible to buyers
Inactive — hidden from buyers
Sold Out — stock is 0
4
Editing & Activating
Edit: tap "Edit" to update details
Deactivate: tap "Deactivate" to hide from buyers
Activate: tap "Activate" to make visible again
View in Store: see how buyers see it
🧾
3. Managing Orders
▼
1
Viewing Orders
Seller Hub → Seller Orders
See all incoming orders with status badges
2
Order Statuses & Actions
Waiting for Seller → Accept or Decline
Pending Payment → wait for buyer proof
Pending Verification → Confirm or Reject proof
Proof Rejected → wait for buyer re-upload
Pending Delivery → deliver the order
Completed → order finished
3
Accepting an Order (COD)
Open the order.
Tap "Accept Order".
Status becomes "Pending Delivery".
Deliver the product.
4
Declining an Order
Open the order.
Tap "Decline Order".
Select a reason (Out of stock, Seller unavailable, etc.).
Confirm.
5
Confirming Payment (Bank Transfer)
Buyer uploads proof of payment.
Review the proof.
Tap "Confirm Payment" if valid.
Or tap "Reject Proof" with a reason.
6
Completing an Order
After delivery, open the order.
Tap "Mark Completed".
Status becomes "Completed".
⚙️
4. Store Settings
▼
1
Editing Store Information
Seller Hub → Store Settings
Edit: Store Name, Category, Description
Update: Contact Number and Email
2
Store Logo
Tap "Store Logo".
Choose Camera or Gallery.
Upload your logo.
Logo appears on your store and product pages.
3
Payment Methods
Toggle COD on/off
Toggle Bank Transfer on/off
If Bank Transfer: enter bank details
4
Batch Update
When you change your Store Name
All active products are automatically updated
Buyers see the new name everywhere
📊
5. Business Overview
▼
1
Dashboard Stats
Products Published — total active products
Orders Received — total orders
2
Viewing Your Store
Tap "View My Store" to see your public store page
Only active products are shown
💰
6. Payments & Payouts
▼
1
How Payments Work
COD: you receive cash directly from buyer
Bank Transfer: buyer transfers to your bank account
2
Verifying Bank Transfers
Buyer transfers money.
Buyer uploads proof.
You verify the proof.
Confirm payment if valid.
3
Rejecting Proof
Tap "Reject Proof".
Select a reason (Payment not received, Incorrect amount, Proof unclear, Wrong reference).
Buyer will re-upload.
⚖️

▼
1
Product Rules

2
Order Rules

3
Account Rules

No review manipulation
No harassment of buyers
Follow all applicable laws
4
Consequences

Account suspension
Permanent ban
